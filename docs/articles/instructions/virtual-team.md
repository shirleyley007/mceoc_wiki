# Virtual Team

Virtual teams let you select members from across the entire team and view their schedules together. They are especially useful for short-term projects: bring the project members into one group, then use their schedules to find suitable times for project meetings.

## Create a Virtual Team

1. On the **Team View** page, click **Virtual Teams** in the toolbar and start creating a new team.
2. Enter a **Team name**.
3. Use the **Owners** and **Members** tabs to manage the group. Select people from the **Active users** list to add them.
4. By default, a virtual team is visible only to its owners. Select **Visible to members** if you also want its members to see it.
5. Click **Save**.

![Create a virtual team and configure its owners, members, and visibility](/virtual-team-create.png)

## View Members' Schedules

1. Open the **Virtual Teams** filter on the **Team View** page.
2. Select your virtual team, such as **MCEOC v-team** in the example below.
3. Click **Apply**.

![Select a virtual team in the schedule filter](/virtual-team-filter.png)

The calendar displays the selected group's schedules together, making it easier to compare availability and plan project meetings.

![Team View showing the selected virtual team's schedules](/virtual-team-schedules.png)

## Validity and Renewal

A virtual team is valid for **one year** by default. Use **Extend** before expiration to renew its validity period.

::: warning Expiration
Expired virtual teams are automatically and permanently deleted. Extend any team you still need before its expiration date.
:::
